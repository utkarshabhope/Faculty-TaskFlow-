Problem Identification & Research Grounding

Academic institutions face chronic inefficiencies due to decentralized communication and lack of auditability. Implementing a transparent Workload Allocation Model (Hull, 2006) resolves:

  • Central Authority & Clear Assignment: The HOD defines milestones and delegates tasks with objective deadlines.
  • Staff Self-Management: Individual faculty can create and track self-assigned academic deliverables.
  • Accreditation Readiness: Continuous compliance data for NBA Criterion 5 and NAAC Criteria 2.
